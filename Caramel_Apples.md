Supplies:

    aluminum foil
    chopsticks or caramel apple sticks
    candy thermometer
    small plates, one for each apple, refrigerated
    parchment paper (for leftover caramel)
    ice water bath
    
Instant Pot Caramel apple dip:

    95g brown sugar
    100g granulated sugar
    1 tsp ground cinnamon
    114 g salted butter
    120ml corn syrup
    155ml sweetened condensed milk (AKA 7oz or 1/2 cup)
    1 tsp vanilla

Prepping the apples:

1. Gather medium to large size unblemished apples. Granny smith, honeycrisp, and other firm apples are your best option, but we had success with galas too.
2. Make a large bowl of icewater and set aside
3. To a pot of boiling water, add 2 TBSP white vinegar and 2 TBSP baking soda. Make sure your pot has enough extra headroom to accommodate the minor eruption.
4. Dip apples in boiling water for 10sec, leaving a small (2in or so) ring at the top of the apple intact. See timestamp 2:45 of the video for more info.
5. Pat the hot water off of the apple with a clean towel, then immediately dunk in the ice bath. Pat dry once more.
6. You will see how the apple becomes discoloured where the wax was removed- this is normal, and the reason why the upper ring is left intact.
7. Prep the rest of the apples.
8. Refrigerate apples for a few hours before dipping. Some juice may leach out of the skin- this is normal, as the wax has been removed.

Making the dip:

1. In a bowl that fits inside your Instant Pot (I use a thick Pyrex bowl), carefully mix ingredients together. Avoid splashing sugar on sides of bowl.
2. Cover tightly with aluminum foil. I also use a rubber band to keep it on.
3. Place trivet in bottom of Instant Pot or pressure cooker.
4. Add enough water to pot to come up on sides of bowl to level of caramel mixture. (this is important, if not high enough all the sugar won’t dissolve and could cause your caramel dip to be grainy).
5. Add sling (I make mine out of aluminum foil)- this will help you remove the bowl when it’s done cooking.
6. Place sealed caramel mixture into Instant Pot. Set valve to "seal."
7. Cook on MANUAL HIGH pressure for 30-40 minutes.
8. Allow 10-minute natural release.
9. Remove bowl. Remove aluminum foil. Add vanilla and Stir until smooth. The mixture will look gelatin-like and clumpy until it is stirred. This is normal. Once stirred it will be smooth.
10. Allow caramel dip to cool and thicken before serving.

Coating the apples:

1. Prepare a double boiler setup by placing a large saucepan of water over medium-high heat. Bring to a simmer.
2. Set a clean, dry metal bowl on top of the saucepan and pour the dip inside.
3. Clip a candy thermometer to the side of the bowl.
4. Keep the dip moving. **You'll need to be constantly stirring it!** Scrape the sides as needed.
5. Allow the dip to heat up and thicken- **190F is the ideal dipping temperature**. The dip will cool slightly after dipping each apple, so wait for it to come to temp again.
6. Dip apples, rolling them for even coverage. Allow excess caramel to drizzle off.
7. Immediately plunk onto a cold plate and put in the fridge, if a plain apple is desired, or roll in toppings while the shell is still malleable, then refrigerate.
8. Let cool for 15-20 minutes, then enjoy!

Leftover caramel:

1. If you are lucky enough to end up with leftovers, spread out a large piece of parchment or wax paper on a cool countertop and scrape remaining caramel onto it using a spatula.
2. Sprinkle lightly with sea salt.
3. Allow to cool, then refrigerate to set. Once cold, you'll be able to cut it into candy pieces.
4. Attempt (and fail) to exercise self-restraint.

Notes: Caramel can be placed in a crockpot for constant, even heating. Great for parties!
Leave the candy thermometer in the pot to keep an eye on the temperature- again, you want it right around 190F.
