Spice cake:

    300 g all-purpose flour
    1 teaspoon baking powder
    1 teaspoon baking soda
    1 teaspoon kosher salt
    1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
    1 1/2 tsp allspice
    1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
    300 g light brown sugar
    100 g granulated sugar
    170 g unsalted butter
    120 ml/ 109 g vegetable oil
    3 large eggs, room temperature
    227 g sour cream
    60 ml whole milk

Frosting:

    227 g cream cheese, room temperature
    114 g unsalted butter, room temperature
    1 tablespoon molasses
    800 g confectioners’ sugar

Spice cake method:

1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Spray a 9×13-inch light-colored aluminum pan with nonstick cooking spray or line with parchment paper. Set aside.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, allspice, and nutmeg. Set aside.
3. In a separate large bowl, whisk together the brown sugar and granulated sugar. Add melted butter and oil, whisking until smooth and glossy.
4. Add eggs one at a time, whisking well after each addition.
5. Add sour cream and milk, stirring to combine.
6. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, stirring until just combined. Do not overmix.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and spread it into an even layer with a spatula.
8. Bake for 40-45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs, but no wet batter. If the top starts to brown too quickly, loosely tent the pan with foil around the 25-minute mark.
9. Remove the cake from the oven and place the pan on a wire rack to cool completely.

Frosting method:

1. In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, combine the cream cheese, butter, and molasses.
2. Beat on low speed until the mixture is smooth and fully combined, scraping down the sides of the bowl as needed.
3. Gradually add the confectioners’ sugar, about 1 cup at a time, beating on low until incorporated.
4. Increase the speed to medium and beat until the frosting is creamy, smooth, and spreadable, or it reaches your desired consistency.
