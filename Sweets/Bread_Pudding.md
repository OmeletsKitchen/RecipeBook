Ingredients

    6 slices day-old bread, torn into small pieces
    2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
    1/2 cup raisins (Optional)
    475ml milk
    150g white sugar
    4 large eggs, beaten
    1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
    1 teaspoon vanilla extract

    
Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
2. Place bread pieces into an 8-inch square baking dish. Drizzle melted butter over bread and sprinkle raisins over top.
3. Whisk milk, sugar, eggs, cinnamon, and vanilla together in a medium mixing bowl until well combined.
4. Pour custard over bread and press gently with a fork until the bread is evenly moistened and has absorbed the custard.
5. Bake in the preheated oven until golden brown, the center is set, and the top springs back when lightly pressed, about 45 minutes.
